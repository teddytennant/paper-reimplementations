arXiv:2609.21944, Guiding Agents of Quantum Games to Equilibrium using Matrix Exponential Fixed-Point Iteration (Habibi, Abanto-Leon, Maghsudi). This kernel implements N-player EGW product strategies (eq 12a), tensor-contraction payoffs (eq 23), effective Hamiltonians via partial contraction (Def 1, eqs 25-27), the Gibbs entropy-regularized best response (Thm 2, eq 30), simultaneous MEFPIA with geometric cooling F(k)=alpha^k (Algorithm 1, eqs 31 and 33), a small Kale-style MMWU baseline, and Prisoner's Dilemma mini-experiment 1 with the separable projective mapping of eqs 34-36. It does not implement mini-experiments 2-3, Experiment 2, logarithmic/linear/exponential/adaptive cooling, or the 100-random-init sweep plots.
