arXiv:2609.21780 PointLAM. This repo implements the Laplacian Point Sampler (LPS: deviation plus doubly sorted sampling) and Local Hadamard Aggregator (LHA: transient-grid neighbor aggregation plus residual Hadamard gating), wired as a tiny LAM block with a simplified axis-wise bidirectional selective scan. It does not implement a nuScenes/Waymo detector, OpenPCDet, Hilbert serialization, or a full Mamba.
